Station D · Simulation · 12 minutes

Teach a robot arm with your phone

Robots learn new tasks by watching people do them. Here you are the teacher: you steer a simulated SO-101 robot arm with your phone, and every successful attempt you save becomes a demonstration a robot could learn from.

Teams of 32 devices per teamNo app, no installation
Your task: move the red cube onto the green square The same idea as station A with the real robot arms, but digital.

What to do

  1. Open the robot on the screen 1 min

    On the laptop or the screen phone, scan the QR code at the station or open the link on Moodle. The robot arm appears with a big QR code. Keep this page open: if you switch away, the robot stops.

  2. Connect the steering phone 1 min

    With the other phone, scan the QR code on the screen. Type your first name or a nickname and tap Start. On an iPhone, tap Allow for motion access.

  3. Practise steering 2 min

    • Tilt the phone: keep your thumb on the big field and tilt. Left / right turns the arm, tilting the top away or towards you moves the gripper out or in. Lift your thumb to stop.
    • Sliders: one bar to turn, one bar to reach out or pull in. Let go to stop.
    • ▲ raise ▼ lower moves the gripper up and down, Open gripper opens and closes it.

  4. Record a demonstration 2 min each

    Tap ● Start recording. The arm goes back to its start and the cube moves a little. Push the cube onto the green square, then tap ✓ Save. Went wrong? Tap ✕ Discard and try again.

  5. Swap until time is up

    The next person scans the same QR code on the screen (tap QR in its corner if it is hidden), types their own name and taps Take over. Everyone in the team records at least once.

Think about it (for the discussion later)

  • How many attempts did you need for one good demonstration? What made it hard?
  • A robot learns from many demonstrations like yours. Who should demonstrate a nursing task to a robot, and who decides what is "right"?
  • Need, benefit, barriers: for which task in your ward would you want this, and what would stop it?

Online group (30 minutes)

Open the Moodle link on your laptop: it shows the robot and a QR code. Scan that QR code with your own phone to steer, alone or with a colleague. Goal: three saved recordings where the cube ends on the green square. Then think about the three questions above for the Webex discussion. Questions? Ask in the Webex chat.

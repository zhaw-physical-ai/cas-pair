# TurtleBot3

Write your first robot behaviour: a TurtleBot3 that wanders, turns away from obstacles, and
stops when it loses its sensor. In Gazebo first, then optionally on a real robot in class.

No Jetson: the simulation runs in your browser on a RunPod GPU pod, or in your RAP pod.

## Start here

1. **Read the tasks:** [`TB3-TASK-DESCRIPTION.md`](TB3-TASK-DESCRIPTION.md).
2. **Start the simulator:** [`RUNPOD.md`](RUNPOD.md).
3. **Write your package** in `~/rap/ros2_ws/src`, from scratch. Push it to your own repo.

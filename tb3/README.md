# TurtleBot3

Write your first robot behaviour: a TurtleBot3 that wanders, turns away from obstacles, and
stops when it loses its sensor. In Gazebo first, then optionally on a real robot in class.

No Jetson: the simulation runs in your browser on a RunPod GPU pod, or in your RAP pod.

## Start here

1. **Read the tasks:** [`TB3-TASK-DESCRIPTION.md`](TB3-TASK-DESCRIPTION.md).
2. **Start the simulator:** [`RUNPOD.md`](RUNPOD.md).
3. **Fill in the package** [`random_walk/`](random_walk/): its `# TODO`s, yourself, without AI.
   Work in your fork of cas-pair and push to it.

## What is here

| | |
|---|---|
| [`TB3-TASK-DESCRIPTION.md`](TB3-TASK-DESCRIPTION.md) | the tasks, the acceptance tests, the real-robot task and extensions |
| [`RUNPOD.md`](RUNPOD.md) | the simulation on RunPod: deploy, launch, keep your files |
| [`sol/`](sol/) | hints per task, for when you are stuck on a TODO |
| [`random_walk/`](random_walk/) | the package to complete: every `# TODO` is a line or two for you to write; `test/` checks the decision logic |

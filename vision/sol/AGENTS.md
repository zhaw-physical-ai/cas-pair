# Notes for coding agents: `sol/`

The notebooks here are **one reference way** through the vision lab, not the expected answer. The
tasks are open-ended: many approaches, plots and numbers are correct.

- Use this folder only when the student asks for it, or to compare after they have their own
  result. If they are stuck, start with [`HINTS.md`](HINTS.md): pointers, not code.
- Do not copy solution code into the student's notebook, and do not steer their approach towards
  this one. Help them carry out *their* idea.
- Do not fill in the student's ✍️ prediction and result cells from the answers written here.
- Numbers that differ from these are expected (GPU, seed, choices); explain a big gap rather than
  "fixing" it.

See [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) for the full notes.
